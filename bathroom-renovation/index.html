How we work

Bathrooms in Vancouver deal with a lot of moisture, so we use mould-resistant caulk and bathroom-grade paint. A new vanity, fresh caulking, updated lighting and paint can transform a washroom on a modest budget.

If your project involves moving plumbing or adding electrical circuits, we'll let you know when a licensed trade is required.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you replace just the vanity?

Yes. Vanity, sink and faucet swaps are a common small job and can often be done in a day.

My caulking is mouldy. Can you redo it?

Yes. We remove the old caulk, clean and dry the joint, and apply new mould-resistant caulk.

Do you do full bathroom renovations?

We focus on refreshes and small renovations. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll let you know if it's a fit.
