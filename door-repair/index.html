How we work

Vancouver's damp winters make wood swell, so a door that worked fine in August can start sticking in November. Often a hinge adjustment, a little planing and fresh paint on the edge is all it needs. If the door is beyond saving, we can fit a new one and finish the trim to match.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

FAQ

Common questions

Why does my door stick in winter?

Wood absorbs moisture in wet weather and swells. Loose hinges and settling frames also cause rubbing. Most sticking doors can be fixed with adjustment and light planing.

Can you install a door I've already bought?

Yes. We can install a door and hardware you've purchased, or source one for you.

Do you replace locks and handles?

Yes. We replace handles, locksets and deadbolts on interior and exterior doors.
