Flooring installation in Vancouver

New floors change how a room feels. FixCraft BC installs laminate and luxury vinyl plank flooring in bedrooms, living areas, condos and basements across Metro Vancouver, and finishes the job with baseboards and transitions.

What's included

  • Laminate and luxury vinyl plank (LVP) installation
  • Removal of old carpet or flooring
  • Underlay and moisture barrier where needed
  • Baseboard and quarter-round removal and reinstall or replacement
  • Transition strips between rooms
  • Small floor repairs and plank replacement

How we work

Vinyl plank is a popular choice in Vancouver condos and basements because it handles moisture well. Many stratas have rules about underlay and sound ratings, so check your bylaws and we'll make sure the install meets them.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

FAQ

Common questions

What flooring is best for basements?

Luxury vinyl plank is usually the best choice for basements because it's water-resistant.

Can you install flooring in a condo?

Yes. Let us know your strata's underlay and sound requirements and we'll install to suit.

Do you install baseboards too?

Yes. We can reinstall your existing baseboards or install new ones after the floor is down.
