What affects the cost of painting a room in Vancouver?

Updated November 2026 · FixCraft BC, Metro Vancouver

Short answer: The price of painting a room depends mostly on how much surface there is, how much repair and prep the walls need, and how big the colour change is. Sending photos and rough room sizes is the fastest way to get an accurate written quote.

Room size and ceiling height

More wall area means more paint and more time. High ceilings, stairwells and vaulted rooms take longer because they need ladders or extension poles and more careful cutting-in along the ceiling line.

What's being painted

Walls alone are the simplest job. Ceilings, baseboards, door and window trim, and doors each add time, because trim and doors are painted with a brush and usually need a different sheen. Tell us which surfaces you want painted so the quote matches.

Wall condition and prep

Most of a good paint job is preparation. Nail holes, dents, cracks, old anchor holes and patched areas all need filling, sanding and spot-priming first. Walls with water stains or glossy old paint may need a full primer coat. More repairs mean more time, but they're what make the finish look clean.

Colour change

Going from a light colour to a slightly different light colour can often be done in two coats. Big changes, such as covering a dark feature wall with white, or painting over bright colours, often need primer and an extra coat.

Access and furniture

Empty rooms are faster. In furnished rooms, furniture has to be moved to the centre and covered, and floors protected. In condos, strata rules about elevator bookings, parking and work hours can also affect scheduling.

How to get an accurate quote

  • Take a photo of each wall, including any damage.
  • Note rough room sizes and ceiling height.
  • List what you want painted: walls, ceiling, trim, doors.
  • Mention the colours you're going from and to, if you know them.

Send these with our quote form and we'll reply with a written price.

Common questions

How many coats of paint does a room need?

Usually two coats for an even finish. Large colour changes or patched walls may also need a coat of primer.

Is it cheaper to paint several rooms at once?

Often, yes. Setup, protection and cleanup are shared across the rooms, so painting several rooms in one visit is usually more efficient than separate visits.
