Installing new flooring in a Vancouver condo: strata rules to check first

Updated November 2026 · FixCraft BC, Metro Vancouver

Short answer: Many Metro Vancouver stratas require written approval before hard flooring goes in, and set rules for underlay and noise between suites. Check your bylaws and get approval in writing before you buy flooring.

Why stratas regulate flooring

Replacing carpet with laminate or vinyl plank can make footsteps and impact noise much louder for the suite below. Because of this, many strata corporations have bylaws about hard-surface flooring, especially in concrete high-rises and wood-frame low-rises.

What to look for in your bylaws

  • Approval: whether you need written permission from strata council before installing hard flooring.
  • Underlay or sound rating: some stratas require a specific acoustic underlay or a minimum sound-rating for the floor system.
  • Work hours: when noisy work such as cutting is allowed.
  • Elevator and parking: booking the elevator with protective pads and where trades can park.
  • Disposal: how old carpet and packaging must be removed from the building.

Ask before you buy

Get your strata's requirements in writing before choosing flooring and underlay, because the required underlay can affect which products will work. Your property manager or strata council can provide the bylaws and any flooring application form.

Choosing flooring for a condo

Luxury vinyl plank is a common condo choice because it's water-resistant and comfortable underfoot, and many products are designed to work with acoustic underlays. Laminate works well in dry rooms. We'll install to your strata's requirements and finish with baseboards and transitions.

Common questions

Do I need strata approval to replace carpet with vinyl plank?

In many Metro Vancouver stratas, yes. Check your bylaws or ask your property manager before starting.

What underlay do I need?

It depends on your strata's bylaws. Some name a specific product or sound rating. Get the requirement in writing, and we'll install to match it.
