What's included

  • Patching holes from doorknobs, anchors and TV mounts
  • Repairing cracks in walls and ceilings
  • Fixing nail pops and dented corners
  • Replacing water-damaged drywall once the leak is fixed
  • Texture matching and smooth-wall finishing
  • Priming and painting the repair to blend in

How we work

A good wall repair is about the finish. We tape, mud and sand in thin coats, then prime and paint so the patch disappears. If the whole wall needs paint afterward, we can quote that at the same time.

For water damage, the source of the leak needs to be fixed first. We can replace and refinish the damaged section once it's dry.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

In most cases, yes. We match common textures and smooth finishes so the repair blends in.

Yes. Small repairs are what we do, and you can bundle several small jobs into one visit.

Do you paint after patching?

Yes. We prime and paint the repaired area, or the whole wall if needed for an even finish.
