Built for the wet season

The North Shore gets a lot of rain, which is hard on exterior paint, wood trim, decks and fences, and makes doors swell and stick. We schedule exterior painting for dry stretches and seal bare wood so it lasts. If your doors are sticking, see why doors stick in winter.

Older homes and updates

Many North Shore homes have original trim, doors and bathrooms that just need updating. A washroom or kitchen refresh keeps the layout and updates finishes, fixtures and paint without a full renovation.
