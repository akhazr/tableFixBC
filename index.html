FAQ

Good questions

Is there a minimum job size?

No. Small jobs are what we're built for. Bundle a few items together and you'll get the most out of a visit.

Do you supply materials and paint?

Yes. We can pick up everything needed, or use materials you've already bought. Material costs are listed separately on your quote.

How quickly can you start?

It depends on the season and job size. Your quote will include the earliest available time window.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We handle rental turnovers and ongoing maintenance lists, and can coordinate access with tenants.
