Interior & exterior painting in Vancouver

Whether it's one tired bedroom, a whole suite before you sell, or a fence that has seen too many wet winters, FixCraft BC paints homes, condos and rentals across Metro Vancouver. Most of a good paint job is the prep, so that's where we spend our time.

What's included

  • Interior walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards and doors
  • Condo and apartment painting, including strata-friendly scheduling
  • Exterior trim, doors, fences, decks and siding touch-ups
  • Patching nail holes, dents and cracks before painting
  • Cabinet and vanity painting for a low-cost kitchen or washroom refresh
  • Move-in, move-out and rental turnover painting

How we work

We protect floors and furniture, fill and sand imperfections, prime where it's needed and cut clean lines at ceilings and trim. You choose the colours and sheen; we can pick up the paint or use what you've already bought.

Exterior work in Vancouver depends on the weather. We schedule outdoor painting for dry stretches, usually late spring through early fall, and do interiors year-round.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost to paint a room in Vancouver?

It depends on the room size, ceiling height, wall condition and how many coats are needed. Send a few photos with the quote form and we'll give you a written price.

Do you paint condos?

Yes. We work in condos and apartments and can fit around strata rules for elevators, parking and quiet hours.

When can you paint exteriors?

Exterior painting needs dry weather, so it's usually booked from late spring to early fall. Interior painting can be done any time of year.
