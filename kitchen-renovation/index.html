How we work

Small changes add up: a fresh backsplash, painted cabinets and new handles can make a kitchen feel new in days instead of months. We'll talk through what you want to change, give you a written quote, and keep the kitchen usable for as much of the job as possible.

Some work, such as new circuits or moving plumbing, needs a licensed electrician or plumber. We'll tell you up front if your project needs one.

Every job starts with a written quote, and we leave the space clean when we're done.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a kitchen refresh take?

Most refresh projects such as a backsplash or cabinet painting take a few days. Your quote will include a time estimate.

Can you paint my existing cabinets?

Yes. Cabinet painting is one of the most cost-effective ways to update a kitchen.

Do you help choose materials?

Yes. We can suggest tile, paint and hardware options, or install what you've picked.
